Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

How do I get started?

Send us a WhatsApp message with the service you need. We reply with the documents and details required and the next steps.

Can I request more than one service at once?

Yes. On the Offerings page, add every service you need to your enquiry and send them together in one message.

How much will it cost?

Fees depend on the service and your situation, so we quote after you message us. We confirm the cost before we start any work.

How long does it take?

Timelines depend on the service and the approving office. We give you an expected timeframe when we quote and keep you updated as your application moves.

Can you help if my business is already registered?

Yes. We handle change of directors, clearance tax, VAT registration, vendor numbers and council licenses for existing businesses.
